# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

HR and workforce operations teams in schools, educational institutions, companies, and other organizations. Public-site visitors are evaluating whether WorkPulse can manage their employee lifecycle while preserving organizational boundaries and human decision-making.

## Product Purpose

WorkPulse connects hiring, active employment, and eventual exit through one multi-tenant workforce platform. It gives organizations a continuous operational record across recruitment, employee records, employment and probation, attendance, leave, and employee self-service.

## Positioning

WorkPulse connects lifecycle modules around the employee record instead of presenting isolated HR forms. AI assists resume extraction and requirement matching, while HR staff retain final hiring authority.

## Operating Context

Organizations operate in separate branded workspaces. HR staff manage applicants, employees, attendance, leave, and employment records; employees use self-service capabilities within the same organization boundary.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Implemented: AI-assisted recruitment, applicant tracking, employee records, employment and probation management, attendance, leave management, and Employee Self-Service.

Planned: Payroll, RFID hardware integration, Workforce Analytics, and Exit / Post-Employment.

Preserve hostname routing, tenant resolution, authentication, cookies, organization isolation, public routes, CTA destinations, and H1-H5 business logic.

## Brand Commitments

WorkPulse is professional, institutional, calm, modern, product-focused, and suitable for diverse organization types. The S1 semantic design tokens and public UI primitives are authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working hiring, employee, attendance, leave, Employee Self-Service, organization-branding, tenant-routing, and security implementations. No customer logos, testimonials, or public performance metrics are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Connect the employee lifecycle instead of fragmenting it.
- Keep organizational workspaces and data separate.
- Preserve human authority over AI-assisted hiring.
- Distinguish implemented capabilities from planned work.
- Communicate operational credibility without inflated claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings, and responsive layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
