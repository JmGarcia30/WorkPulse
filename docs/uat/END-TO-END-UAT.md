## Ordered local execution checklist

Record **PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT RUN**, tester, date, tenant/actor, disposable record IDs, expected versus actual result, and redacted evidence for each ID. A blocked scenario is never a pass. Use independent expected monetary calculations prepared by finance.

1. **U01 — environment and disposable actors.** Verify the recovery mapping using the existing recovery configuration/preflight process; stop if the target differs. Confirm the linked B1 postflight before candidate/onboarding tests. Use run prefix `UAT-20261024-<run>` throughout. Plan tenant A from public registration and tenant B from platform creation; use distinct controlled mailboxes, a candidate, A employees E1/E2, B employee E3, HR Administrators, an Organization Administrator and Hiring Manager where existing authorized fixture provisioning permits. Use an authorized platform test operator; tenant credentials must not become platform credentials. Do not run seeds, reset scripts or bare fixture scripts loading `.env`.

2. **U02 — start locally with controlled delivery.** In a dedicated PowerShell process, supply authenticated test SMTP/catcher inputs without changing environment files. Generic SMTP needs nonempty username/password; clear process `GMAIL_USER` and `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD` so legacy Gmail does not take precedence. A catcher does not intercept tenant OAuth Gmail: connect only accounts controlled by the testers. Run:

   ```powershell
   npm run dev
   ```

   Require the launcher message `Using verified disposable workpulse-batch0-recovery`. It pins both database URLs and localhost origins, but retains email inputs. Open `http://localhost:3000/platform`, `http://localhost:3000/register`, and tenant login `http://<slug>.localhost:3000/login`. SAGA entry points are `http://saga.localhost:3000` and `http://localhost:3000/saga`. Do not use bare `next dev`, change production settings or expose the local server publicly.

3. **U03 — platform and public registration.** Register A at `/register`; before verification expect no tenant/login-capable User. Verify in the originating browser, log into its generated workspace, then try wrong browser, reused/expired token, resend and duplicate email. At `/platform`, create B with a unique slug/email and intended timezone; validate invalid/reserved slug, repeat request, invitation delivery/activation/reuse/revoke. Confirm platform details show permitted organization metadata, not workforce records; verify both tenants appear once. Public tenants start in UTC with careers disabled; record the actual timezone, not an assumed Philippine date boundary.

4. **U04 — tenant setup and email.** In each disposable tenant configure branding at `/dashboard/organization`, hiring requirements at `/dashboard/hiring/settings`, probation at `/dashboard/employees/probation-settings`, leave at `/dashboard/leave/settings`, payroll configuration at `/dashboard/payroll`, and resignation policy at `/dashboard/settings/exit`. New tenants must not inherit SAGA rules. Confirm careers remains disabled and record B3: there is no application activation control; do not invent a settings step or silently enable it with SQL. At `/dashboard/settings/email-integration`, connect a controlled Google account, send to an explicit controlled test recipient and confirm inbox/spam receipt; verify wrong-tenant OAuth state, disconnect/reconnect and provider failure handling. Preview each HR email from an applicant before explicitly sending; double-click/retry must not duplicate. Preserve existing SAGA sender/policies: test activation/revert only in a separately authorized disposable SAGA fixture, never toggle existing SAGA settings for this run.

5. **U05 — recruitment.** Create/publish a disposable job at `/dashboard/hiring/jobs`; apply through `/careers/<organizationSlug>/<jobSlug>/apply` with safe synthetic documents. **Still blocked on newly created A/B by B3; B1's schema prerequisite is restored.** Resume after separately reviewed careers resolution, or use an authorized disposable enabled fixture only for downstream coverage. Check required/invalid files, duplicate application and closed job. Verify candidate email access, expiry/reuse/revocation, correct portal and private downloads. A known application ID alone must reveal nothing. Reapply using the controlled candidate identity and confirm public input cannot overwrite another tenant's applicant identity. Record interview, assessment and allowed pipeline transitions at `/dashboard/hiring/applicants` and related tabs. Exercise rejection on a separate candidate.

6. **U06 — hiring, onboarding and employment.** Create the offer, inspect agreed probation/document snapshots, record the existing acceptance flow, initialize onboarding and verify required evidence or documented waiver. Attempt conversion while incomplete: reject without partial employee creation. Complete onboarding at `/dashboard/hiring/onboarding`; convert once, repeat safely and verify one Employee/employment history at `/dashboard/employees/<id>`. Candidate access must be revoked on completion. Run Teaching/Non-Teaching and probation regularization/renewal/non-renewal branches on separate disposable employees; preserve historical agreements and restrict SAGA-specific rules to SAGA. Activate ESS via its controlled invitation; expired/reused/disabled invitations must fail.

7. **U07 — employment, attendance and leave.** As E1 inspect `/employee/profile` and `/employee/employment`; E2/E3 data must be inaccessible. Assign a schedule at `/dashboard/attendance/schedules`; record and evaluate disposable attendance at `/dashboard/attendance`, including normal, late, missing pair, duplicate submission and versioned correction. Confirm `/employee/attendance` agrees. Grant leave through `/dashboard/leave/balances`; request at `/employee/leave/new`, approve/reject/withdraw/cancel separate requests. Check overlap/insufficient balance, ledger debit/reversal once, attendance sync and dashboard notifications. No RFID dependency.

8. **U08 — payroll, before exit.** At `/dashboard/payroll` enter effective-dated compensation, reviewed policy, supported statutory bases and evidenced opening history. Generate supported monthly and conventional semi-monthly cases; compare each earnings/deduction/net and second-cutoff reconciliation with the independent finance worksheet. Missing setup must block, never become zero. Review, finalize, retry generation/edit and confirm immutable amounts; E1 sees only its finalized payslip at `/employee/payroll`. Exercise E2/tenant B denial. **Currently BLOCKED for payment dates after 22 October (B2).** A disposable historical full-month fixed-salary fixture may test supported arithmetic, but cannot substitute for a current-date acceptance pass or the newly hired employee's unsupported partial month.

9. **U09 — resignation and HR processing.** E1 submits at `/employee/resignation` with reason, last working day and effective date. Require valid date ordering; retry/double-submit yields one active case. Submission preserves ESS and creates no final-pay record. HR sees Requires Attention and the same case at `/dashboard/employees/exit`; acknowledgment clears the queue and appears in ESS without a discretionary accept/reject gate. Test configured compliant notice, short notice requiring authorized exception, missing-policy guidance, and SAGA calendar-month/superior/HRMO rules on disposable fixtures. Missing policy must not prevent lodging a notice, but blocks readiness. ESS withdrawal needs employee-request evidence through HR; cancelled history remains and resubmission creates a new case.

10. **U10 — clearance and dated execution.** Clear or explicitly waive all required items, enter applicable documentary reviews and resolve readiness blockers. Pending/invalidated evidence, stale revisions and invalid transitions must fail safely. Approve; a future effective date retains employment/ESS. Execute explicitly on/after the organization's effective date: expect COMPLETED/SEPARATED, closed employment and disabled ESS/invitations. Retried execution must have no duplicate effects. Test conflicting post-boundary attendance and separate HR termination/non-renewal cases with their own grounds/reviews. Do not use a browser clock override or backdate genuine records: schedule the disposable resignation over the necessary real dates; use separately reviewed historical fixtures only for labeled regression branches.

11. **U11 — final pay and retained history.** In the existing Exit detail, create one draft and review earned salary, proportionate 13th month, applicable leave conversion, ground-specific separation pay, additions and lawful deductions. Missing or unsupported evidence leaves totals undetermined. No universal separation-pay entitlement or automated final tax is assumed. Draft/review can precede exit; **approval requires completed separation**. Change source evidence before approval and require renewed review; explicitly approve as HR Administrator. Organization Administrator is read-only for final-pay mutation. Reject finalized edits, duplicate record, overpayment and duplicate reference/retry; record partial then full payment with date/reference/remarks. Verify deadline/overdue and paid-late displays, shorter organization terms and preserved deadline basis. Payment pending must never prevent/reverse lawful execution. Confirm historical payroll, leave, attendance, employee and audits remain readable by authorized HR; old ESS sessions and invitations fail.

12. **U12 — security sweep and sign-off.** Repeat foreign IDs/tenant fields, another employee's IDs and direct action/document requests for every stage; expect no data or mutation. Verify Hiring Manager restrictions, HR-only policy/final-pay changes, disabled accounts, stale-role sessions, platform/tenant session separation and forged Host/forwarded-host denial. Inspect HTML/RSC/prefetch bodies as well as redirect status; no private names, financial details, documents, credentials or tokens may leak. Use two browser sessions for racing notices, conversion, approval/execution and payments; one legitimate outcome, no duplicate effects. Reconcile final counts/amounts/audits and dashboard/analytics visibility. Retain signed evidence and disposable history; close/archive only through existing workflows, never delete finalized/audit records or clean up by SQL/reset.

## Coverage gaps and security interpretation

- Existing module evidence is substantial, but no reviewed report demonstrates one freshly executed browser journey through all stages with the same employee, current-date payroll, and real email. Leave/probation policy UI and the latest ESS/final-pay responsive flows still need human acceptance; HTTP/SSR checks are not visual browser testing.
- Rollback adapters use serialized savepoints. They do not establish independent committed races for the latest resignation/final-pay flows. H10 adapters omit the native TRUNCATE assertion/independent attendance-lock race and substitute client-boundary audit-failure injection. Preserve these limitations when recording results.
- Historical `FINDINGS.json` still lists CD-01/CD-05 as open; the later candidate identity report and current credential predicates supersede those entries. Profile stale-role response disclosure was also fixed/retested. B1 was a missing schema prerequisite for the corrected code and is now restored on recovery, not evidence the old access bypass is still exposed.
- **Confirmed security limitation:** tenant staff JWTs last seven days; logout deletes the browser cookie without server-side token revocation. A copied token can remain valid while its persisted user/role/tenant still match; restoring an earlier role/membership can revalidate an unexpired token. Record security-owner acceptance or remediation disposition before production sign-off. ESS additionally checks current account/employment eligibility, so this does not negate separation access revocation. See [session implementation](../../src/lib/auth/session.ts) and [security follow-up](../functional-regression/profile-security/REPORT.md).
- Hosted private Blob authorization/persistence, live provider faults and internet-wide registration/mail abuse resistance remain unverified operational/security coverage. No fresh dependency/advisory scan or penetration test was performed; this inspection is not a security certification.

## Migration and production sign-off

The initial read-only catalog comparison found **35 local migrations, 34 applied, 67 application tables, no unfinished non-rolled-back migration, no unknown applied migration, and no checksum mismatch** (allowing Prisma line-ending variants). The sole recovery gap was Candidate Access, subsequently corrected under separate authorization; consult the linked Blocker 1 report for postflight results. Additive Hiring, Probation, statutory payroll, platform/onboarding/public registration, organization Gmail/HR delivery, resignation policy, final pay and ESS notice migrations are already recorded on recovery, through `20261024000500_ess_resignation_notice`. This does not prove complete schema drift absence or production parity.

Before any separately authorized production release, compare the actual production ledger, schema/constraints/triggers and reviewed migration files; approve the exact pending set, backup and rehearsal on a disposable production-like copy. Apply no blanket migration bundle based only on recovery's pending list. Resolve B2 and independently review supported payroll inputs/histories. Verify canonical owned root/wildcard DNS, HTTPS and callback URLs, trusted forwarding, stable secrets/encryption key, real neutral registration/invitation sender, private Blob token/persistence and authorized platform operator access. Production environment and provisioning remain unverified here.

Unsupported payroll/final-tax/separation-pay cases, automatic bank transfers, post-finalization payment corrections/reversals, ESS notice self-editing, and arbitrary custom domains are scope limits rather than newly invented defects. Determine whether actual UAT scenarios need any of them; do not silently mark unsupported scenarios passed or introduce features during this audit. Final pay's displayed Philippine deadline basis needs business/legal acceptance for the actual covered cases; no new legal/rate assumptions were introduced here.

UAT completion requires every applicable checklist item to have evidence, no unresolved functional/security blocker, explicit disposition of scope limits, and HR, finance, tenant/security and platform-owner sign-off. Production operational gates remain separate from local UAT acceptance.
