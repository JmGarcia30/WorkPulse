Reimbursement Claims Abroad and 
Premium Contributions
Overseas  Filipino  Workers  (OFWs)  and  dependents  wherever  they  are  abroad  
are entitled to avail benefits through reimbursement of confinements overseas 
of  applicable  PhilHealth  All  Case  Rate.  Claims  for  reimbursement  of  overseas  
confinements  should  be  filed  within  one  hundred  eighty  (180)  days  from  the  
date of discharge. When they are in the Philippines, they may also avail of other 
PhilHealth  benefits  and  services  including,  the  Yaman  ng  Kalusugan  Program  
(YAKAP) and the Z benefits from contracted providers.
To ensure the sustainability of the PhilHealth benefits and programs, R.A. 11223 
otherwise known as Universal Health Care (UHC) Act, provides that all members 
shall contribute to the fund, while PhilHealth Circular No. 2020-0005 (Revision 1) 
provides the premium contribution schedule in the National Health Insurance 
Program.  Direct  Contributors  including  Overseas  Filipino  Workers  (OFWs)  are  
required to pay the premium contributions equivalent to five percent (5.0%) of 
the monthly income effective January 2025.
Premium   contributions   can   be   paid   monthly,   quarterly,   semi-annually,   or   
annually to all PhilHealth accredited collecting agents both local and overseas.

Members  are  advised  to  verify  the  posting  of  their  premium  contributions  
through the PhilHealth Member Portal or the eGov PH Super App.
OFs    are  encouraged  to  keep  their  PhilHealth  Member  Records  updated,  
including their contact information, to ensure the timely receipt of PhilHealth 
advisories  and  facilitate  access  to  PhilHealth  benefits  and  services.  Member  
record  updating  may  be  done  through  any  PhilHealth  Local  Health  Insurance  
Office (LHIO) or through the PhilHealth Member Portal at https://memberinquiry.
philhealth.gov.ph/member/.
For  inquiries  on  contribution  payments  and  benefit  availment,  OFs  and  their  
dependents  may  visit  nearest  PhilHealth  Regional  and  Local  Health  Insurance  
Offices in the Philippines through the assigned PhilHealth Account Information 
Management Specialist for Overseas Filipinos (POFs) or contact the PhilHealth 
Corporate Action Center at (02) 8662-2588 or actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph.
